Common questions

I don't know the part number. Can you still make the wire?

Yes. Send the old wire, or its length and end fittings, and we make it to match.

What should I send with my request?

A photo of the wire, or the wire itself, is best. Add the machine brand and model, the length, the end fittings, the color and the sheath if you know them.

Do you supply distributors?

Yes. We supply distributors with delivery and shipping. Tell us what you stock and which machines you serve, and we will quote.

Can you make wires in a specific color or sheath?

Yes. We make wires in the colors, sheaths and finishes you ask for.

What does the warranty cover?

Two years against manufacturing defects, under MS CABLES' approved terms. Warranty terms are available on request.
